Use case: logo-brand. Produce ONE professional brand identity exploration board containing SIX fundamentally DIFFERENT new minimalist logo symbols for 东方智慧 / EASTERN WISDOM, an Eastern wisdom learning and personal growth app. Product essence: credible Eastern philosophy becomes practical daily companionship, helping people KNOW THEMSELVES, SETTLE AND CARE FOR BODY AND MIND, and GROW THROUGH LEARNING AND PRACTICE. Three gateways: life exploration, bodily awareness, mind/emotional awareness. The product includes trusted teachers, reading and courses, meditation, journaling and AI assistance; it is a unified self-growth platform, not a music-only app, hospital, fortune telling service or religious sect. CRITICAL correction: the prior attempts wrongly copied a logo of nested breathing rings and a gold dot. Absolutely NO nested concentric rings, no arc-and-dot variations, no swirl, no gold dot, no fingerprint, no wifi symbol, no generic infinity loop. Simplicity is the only thing to retain, not any former silhouette. Develop six DISTINCT concept-driven silhouettes, not stylistic variations of one motif. Presentation: 3 columns x 2 rows, generous white margins, pure white background, dark jade green solid ink (#284d43) only, tiny clean numerical labels 01 02 03 04 05 06 underneath. No other text. Same optical size and polished flat vector-like craftsmanship. Each logo uses at most TWO or THREE simple solid shapes or FOUR broad clean strokes, no fine detail, no shading, no 3D, no gradients, no texture; should work as a single-color stamp and at 24px. Six concept briefs: 01 SELF DISCOVERY / a minimal sculptural open doorway whose deliberate negative-space cut suggests a person looking inward and a clear path; iconic asymmetrical architectural silhouette, not a literal drawn door with a dot head. 02 THREE GATEWAYS ONE GROWTH / three short simple geometric paths unite into one upward movement, compact balanced custom mark expressing life body mind working together; no trident, no lotus petals, no leaves. 03 WISDOM INTO DAILY LIFE / two clean folded page-like planes create a subtle upward passage in negative space; an abstract open-book wisdom mark with no outlines or page lines, recognizable and distinctive. 04 EASTERN ROOTS MODERN WISDOM / an original compact square seal-like mark using only three or four broad geometric strokes loosely informed by the structure of 東 without attempting to typeset the whole Chinese character; open balanced counters, clean contemporary icon, not ornamental calligraphy, not a religious symbol. 05 BODY MIND HARMONY / two unequal complementary simple sculpted solid forms fit together around a calm upright open negative space; balance and human warmth, no circle container, no yin-yang dots, no heart icon. 06 SMALL PRACTICE CONTINUOUS GROWTH / a compact single continuous ascending folded path with one deliberate open notch expressing step-by-step learning and return to self; only two broad planes, distinctive monolithic silhouette, not a generic finance arrow. All six must be radically different by outer silhouette and conceptual construction. Eastern cultural calm, humane contemporary digital brand, mature and refined. Avoid illustrative mountains, trees, flowers, anatomical bodies, circuit boards, medical crosses, decorative borders, app icon squares and mockups. This is a new concept board based on the PRODUCT, not an edit of an existing logo.
